## Reviewed scope

## Review findings closed before freeze

The implementation review history found and corrected substantive authority defects rather than waiving them:

- forged or weakened caller-constructed policy evaluation could not remain an approval authority input; issuance now consumes opaque Control-owned trusted review material;
- revocation and current time could not remain caller-selected validation inputs; Control owns authoritative revocation and time;
- wall-clock rollback could not revive expired evidence; authority time is monotonic/fail-closed;
- retained-review bounds must be checked before cloning hostile material;
- expired/revoked live entries are reclaimed into bounded replay tombstones instead of exhausting daemon capacity;
- wire review decoding rejects approval classes weaker than reviewed risk, protected `allow`, `blocked`/allow mismatch, and `change-proposed` with read-only reviewed risk;
- CLI plan-review/explanation preserves semantic reason summary and intent/requirement/capability origins;
- D-Bus authentication remains distinct from human/admin approval and models/agents remain untrusted proposers.
