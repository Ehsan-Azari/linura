## Trust and authority interpretation

v0.4 proves a durable **authority transaction/recovery substrate**, not managed mutation support. `Prepared`, `Indeterminate`, `Verified`, policy `Allow`, approval evidence, transaction/generation identity, persisted audit state and the process-local dispatch permit contract are not public mutation credentials.

The released roadmap state remains:

- `executor_state = "none"`;
- `managed_mutation_support = "none"`;
- `complete_lifecycle = false`.

Raw filesystem/database writers can alter bytes, but cannot mint valid keyed retained-record integrity tags without the separately provisioned integrity key. Coherent rollback of the whole database/filesystem/VM snapshot remains unsupported without a future independently protected monotonic anchor/restore protocol.
