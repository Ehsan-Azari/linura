## Trust and authority interpretation

The v0.4 evidence proves a durable **authority transaction/recovery substrate**, not managed mutation support. `Prepared`, `Indeterminate`, `Verified`, committed metadata, transaction/generation identity, policy Allow and approval evidence remain distinct from execution authority. The repository still records `executor_state = none`, `managed_mutation_support = none`, `complete_lifecycle = false` for v0.4.

Raw filesystem/database writers are not prevented from modifying bytes. The claim is that unauthorized retained-record changes fail closed because valid keyed integrity tags cannot be minted without the separately provisioned integrity key. Coherent rollback of the whole database/filesystem/VM snapshot remains unsupported without a future independent monotonic anchor/restore protocol.
